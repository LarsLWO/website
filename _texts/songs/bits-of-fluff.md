In Africa there walks a gnu,
a lion yawns, enjoys the view.
He stops—and there, beneath his nose,
a bit of fluff clings to his toes!

In Australia, skies are blue,
here comes a bouncing kangaroo!
Inside her pouch, tucked out of sight,
a lonely fluffball’s sitting tight!

In Canada, the snow falls slow,
across the lake the skaters go.
But on your hood, what’s hanging there?
A bit of fluff, without a care!

In China, dragons twist and sway,
as dancers leap and laugh and play.
But something else is spinning round—
a bit of fluff won’t touch the ground!

In France, I buy a warm baguette,

In Germany, they pass the ball,
“Goooal! Black, red and gold!” they call.
Then in their shoes—what do they see?
A bit of fluff! How can that be?
